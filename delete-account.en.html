Delete your ADVMAP account

Version 1.0 — 17 September 2026 · Français

You can delete your ADVMAP account yourself, at any time, without writing to us and without giving a reason.

Before you delete

Remember to export your data first: the same "Your data" section lets you download everything in a readable file. After deletion it is too late.

How to do it

  1. Open ADVMAP and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings, section "Your data".
  3. Choose "Delete my account", then confirm by typing your username.

What is deleted

Your profile, your tracks and their GPX files, your photos, your posts, your comments, your reviews and your messages. Deletion is immediate and final: nothing can be restored afterwards. Our host's technical backups may keep a copy for a few days before being overwritten in turn.

What remains

A message you have sent stays visible to the person you sent it to, on their device, if they have already received it — no messaging service can undo that.

Cannot access the app?

Write to advmap@sisilab.app from the email address linked to your account, asking for deletion. We handle the request within one month.
