Delete some of your ADVMAP data

Version 1.6 — 4 October 2026 · Français

Without deleting your account, you can erase a large part of what you have published on ADVMAP yourself, at any time. For the rest, write to us.

What you can delete yourself

  • A track: Profile tab, in your list of tracks, tap Delete then confirm with a second tap. The track is erased along with its GPX file and photos.
  • A post: open it, then "Delete post". Its photos are erased with it.
  • A comment: open the post and delete your comment.
  • A review: on the track page, "Delete my review".
  • An event you created: open it, then "Delete event". Its cover photo and chat are erased with it.
  • A recorded ride, or a "My version" track: these are private tracks, deleted like any other from the Profile tab.
  • A trail report: Settings > Your data > "My reports", or during navigation from "Report on the trail". Either way, it erases itself 37 days after it was made.
  • A block: Settings > Blocked people, then "Unblock".
  • Your bio and motorcycle details: edit or clear them from your profile.

What happens next

Deletion is immediate and final: nothing can be restored. Attached files — GPX file, photos — are erased together with the content. Our host's technical backups may keep a copy for a few days before being overwritten in turn.

What you cannot delete from the app

Your private messages, one by one, and your reports to moderation. To have them erased, write to advmap@sisilab.app from the email address linked to your account, saying what you want deleted. We handle the request within one month. Your download history erases itself after 60 days. Your email address and identifier cannot be erased without deleting the account, since they are what you sign in with.

Delete everything

To delete your account and all of your data, follow the account deletion page: https://advmap.sisilab.app/delete-account.html
